First tapThe reading page

Acts 2:42

They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers.

What the words are doing

"Devoted themselves" is one verb, προσκαρτεροῦντες, in a form that means they kept it up over time. It governs all four items, so this is one habit seen from four sides rather than four separate activities.

"The prayers" has the definite article. Not prayer in general, but a known set of them.

What was happening

Jerusalem, within weeks of Pentecost. These are Jewish believers who still went up to the temple at the fixed hours of prayer and then ate in each other's houses. Nothing here has left the synagogue yet.

Readingwritten by the model

The four items are probably not a programme Luke is prescribing. He is describing what the community looked like, and the single governing verb is what makes it a portrait rather than a checklist.
