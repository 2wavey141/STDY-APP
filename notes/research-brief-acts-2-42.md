**This is not app content.** It is the editorial thinking from the design

## Where and when · *needs curated historical sources*

- **The hours of prayer.** Jewish practice fixed prayer at set times, including
  the hour of the afternoon offering. Acts 3:1 has Peter and John going up at
  the ninth hour. — *claimed from Mishnah, Tamid 5 and Josephus, Antiquities
  14.65. Both are public domain and quotable at length, so this is verifiable
  properly rather than merely cited.*
- **Houses, not buildings.** No church buildings existed. Acts 2:46 has them in
  the temple courts by day and breaking bread at home. A well-off Jerusalem
  house could hold thirty or forty in its courtyard. — *claimed from Avigad,
  Discovering Jerusalem. Modern work: name the position, cite the page, never
  reproduce the prose. Page unverified.*
- **How bread was opened.** The head of the household took the loaf, blessed
  God, tore it, passed it round. Flat loaves were torn, never cut. — *no source
  was ever attached to this. Either find one or drop it.*

## See it · *needs the image library (track A)*

- Southern steps of the Temple Mount — the main pilgrim approach to the courts.
- The Burnt House — a first-century Jerusalem house excavated with rooms intact.
- Stone oven and bread stamp — daily home baking, flat loaves.
- Model of the Second Temple — how the courts, steps and city fit together.

Places and objects, never scenes. Each needs its licence checked individually
and provenance recorded.

## The words · *needs Strong's and MorphGNT ingested*

- **προσκαρτεροῦντες** — to persist in, attend constantly. Claimed ten NT uses,
  six in Acts. Paired with ἦσαν for a sustained habit. *Strong's G4342.*
- **κοινωνίᾳ** — from κοινός, common. Claimed nineteen uses. Participation,
  partnership in a shared work, and literally a cash contribution.
  *Strong's G2842.*
- **κλάσει τοῦ ἄρτου** — a breaking. Claimed only two NT uses, both by Luke:
  here and Luke 24:35. *Strong's G2800.*
- **ταῖς προσευχαῖς** — prayers, plural, with the article. A known set rather
  than prayer in general. *Strong's G4335.*

Every count above is checkable by machine once MorphGNT is in, which makes this
the cheapest facet to make genuinely sourced. Do it before the prose ones.

## Where it sits · *partly verifiable today*

- **One of three summaries.** Luke stops the narrative three times to describe
  the community: Acts 2:42-47, 4:32-35, 5:12-16. — *these are scripture
  references and we already hold the text. Verifiable now.*
- **It answers the sermon.** Sits directly after Peter's Pentecost sermon and
  the response, Acts 2:14-41. — *verifiable now.*
- **Volume two of one work.** Luke 1:1-4 and Acts 1:1 address the same reader.
  — *verifiable now.*
- **Four items, one verb.** The four datives hang off a single periphrastic
  imperfect. — *needs MorphGNT to show rather than assert.*

## Read next · *needs Treasury of Scripture Knowledge*

- **Luke 24:35** — the road to Emmaus, known to them in the breaking of bread.
  The only other use of that noun. *Claim checkable once MorphGNT is in.*
- **Acts 4:32** — the second summary. One heart and soul, everything in common.
- **1 Corinthians 10:16** — Paul uses κοινωνία of the cup and the bread. The
  closest bridge to the Lord's Supper.
- **Acts 6:4** — the apostles apply the community's own verb to themselves.
