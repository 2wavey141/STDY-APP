A visualizer for deep Bible study

One subject at the centre, six facets orbiting it: where and when it happened, what the words are doing, what you can look at, and where scholars still disagree. Each prototype is a phone screen you can tap through.

Interface prototypes only. No real data behind them yet, and every citation is an unverified placeholder.

How to read the colours.
  • Gold is retrieved — a lexicon, a morphology database, a cross-reference set, a named commentary. Cited and checkable.
  • Violet is written by the model, always reasoned from the gold above it, never floating free.

What to look at.

  • Whether you would tap past the reading page at all, or stop at the first screen.
  • Whether the orbit tells you anything a ranked list wouldn't.
  • Whether the split between sourced fact and model interpretation reads clearly without being explained.
  • Whether still argued — naming the positions instead of flattening them into one answer — is the reason you would come back.
