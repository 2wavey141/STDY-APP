A visualizer for deep Bible study

Three working prototypes, in the order they were designed. Each one is a phone screen you can tap through. Everything is built around a single verse, Acts 2:42.

Interface prototypes only. No real data behind them yet.

Latest

Verse visualizer

One verse at the center and six bubbles around it: setting, structure, the Greek words, things you can look at, where to read next, and the questions scholars have not settled. Tap a bubble and it rotates to the top.

One verse, two depths

A plain reading page on the first tap, and a map of related passages on the second. Side by side, to show that the depth is optional. Sourced facts are marked in red, model-written interpretation in blue.
